Booking information

Before you book

Do I need an account to place an order?

Yes. Sign in or create a customer account to save your booking and follow order updates.

Is the amount shown at booking final?

The booking total is an estimate based on current service pricing and configured fees. If the team adjusts the final price after assessing the laundry, the updated amount is shown in your order.

How are pickup times selected?

Choose from the available pickup windows shown in the portal. Availability depends on the business schedule and remaining capacity, and is checked again when you submit the booking.

Can I pay online?

Online card payments are not currently processed by this portal. Payment is handled separately by the business, and the order is marked paid only when payment is verified.
